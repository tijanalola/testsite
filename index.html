Frequently asked questions

Is this a good fit for me? ⌄

That depends on your practice. It can include the systems around enquiries, onboarding, bookings, forms, invoicing, email, files, and internal workflows.

What exactly do I get? ⌄

You receive a connected setup, simple documentation, and training so you can confidently use what has been built.

You receive a clear recommendation for the next best step for your practice.

What if I'm not good with technology? ⌄

You do not need to be. Everything is explained in plain language and built to be manageable.

What if I already have a system but it's messy? ⌄

That is a very common place to start. We can bring order to what you have before adding anything new.
